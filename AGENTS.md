# Contribution and evaluation guidelines

The project demonstrates trustworthy package tracking and problem resolution. Follow the project scope and official competition rules.

## Engineering requirements

- Treat every package claim according to its source. A carrier-delivered scan is not proof of customer receipt.
- Keep fictional events, camera reconstructions, and device placeholders explicitly labeled.
- Preserve auditable action results, including failures, retries, and proof of successful changes.
- Protect privacy: never commit private customer records, credentials, access tokens, or production data.
- Require authorization and server-side access checks for real customer or management operations.
- Maintain compatibility with the documented MCP protocol and provide repeatable installation and test commands.
- Keep the UI readable on mobile, with accessible controls and clear status messages.
- Do not claim a real Ring connection, Alexa device, or carrier submission without evidence from a working authorized integration.
- Document feature changes and run relevant checks before describing them as verified.

## Release evaluation

Demonstrate a working application, truthful source attribution, and a reproducible setup. Do not expose the local prototype to a public network: it is not a production multi-user system. The competition's official requirements take precedence over this guidance.
